Who We Are

About Fresh Track

Fresh Track is a student-led innovation startup founded under the Junior Achievement (JA) Company Programme 2026 in Mauritius. We build intelligent, AI-powered kitchen management solutions that empower everyday households to manage groceries effortlessly.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify household food tracking by combining AI technology with everyday kitchen habits. By providing smart receipt scanning, real-time expiry tracking, and custom recipe suggestions, we make sustainable living convenient, affordable, and accessible to every family.

Target Objective

Reach 1,000 active households in Mauritius within Year 1.

Our Core Purpose

We exist to solve edible food waste right at the source: the home. We believe no fresh food should end up in a landfill simply because it was forgotten in the back of a refrigerator. Fresh Track turns food waste into financial savings and healthy meals.
